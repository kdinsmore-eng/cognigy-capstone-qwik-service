Annual safety inspection

Most vehicles must pass a safety inspection once every twelve months. A certified inspector checks the safety items, and a passing vehicle gets a new windshield sticker. John can walk you through the process on the call and book you at a certified center.

  • Brakes & steering
  • Lights & horn
  • Tires & wheels
  • Glass, belts & airbags
12months between inspections for most vehicles
15days to repair listed defects and return for reinspection if the vehicle doesn't pass
1windshield sticker. The large number is the expiration year.

Frequently asked

Can I change my service center location?

Yes. You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to choose your services or time again.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is usually sent by email, and text reminders may be available.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10–15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service center while booking.

Can I book if I'm not sure what my vehicle needs?

Yes. Choose a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss the symptoms with the technician.
